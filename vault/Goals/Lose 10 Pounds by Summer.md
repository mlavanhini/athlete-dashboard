Starting weight: 186 lbs (Jan 2026). Target: 176 lbs by July 1, 2026. Currently at 181 lbs.

## Why this matters

Race weight. Every pound lighter = ~2 seconds/mile improvement in marathon pace. Also general health and energy.

## Approach

- Calorie deficit of ~300-400 kcal/day on non-run days
- High protein (preserves muscle)
- Not sacrificing training fuel — this is a slow cut
- Working with [[Jamie Rodriguez]] to ensure performance isn't affected
