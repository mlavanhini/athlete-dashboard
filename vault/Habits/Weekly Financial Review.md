# Weekly Financial Review

Every Friday at 5pm. Check accounts, categorize transactions, review spending vs. budget, update net worth tracker.

## Current streak: 6 weeks

## Checklist

- [ ] Review checking and savings balances
- [ ] Categorize any uncategorized transactions
- [ ] Check credit card balance (pay if needed)
- [ ] Update net worth spreadsheet
- [ ] Note any financial decisions needed next week

## Time: ~20 minutes
