# Meditation

10 minutes each morning after coffee, before run. Headspace app (unguided sessions).

## Current streak: 15 days

## Practice

- Sit quietly, focus on breath
- When mind wanders, return without judgment
- End with 3 intentions for the day

## Notes

Noticed significant improvement in stress response at work when I maintain 10+ day streaks. Related to [[Mindfulness & Focus]] topic.
