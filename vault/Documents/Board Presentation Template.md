# Board Presentation Template

Standard template for quarterly board financial presentations.

## Slide structure

1. **Executive summary** — 3 bullets: revenue, EBITDA, cash
2. **Revenue** — actual vs. budget vs. prior year, by segment
3. **Expenses** — total and by category, variance analysis
4. **EBITDA bridge** — waterfall from budget to actual
5. **Cash flow** — operating, investing, financing
6. **Balance sheet highlights** — AR days, AP days, inventory turns
7. **KPI dashboard** — 6 key metrics with targets
8. **Outlook** — reforecast for remainder of year
9. **Appendix** — detailed P&L, department breakdowns

## Design notes

- Dark background, minimal text per slide
- One key number per slide, large and prominent
- Consistent amber/dark color scheme
