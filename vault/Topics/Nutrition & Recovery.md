# Nutrition & Recovery

Endurance sports nutrition, recovery protocols, and sleep optimization.

## Daily targets

- Calories: ~3,200 on training days / ~2,600 on rest days
- Protein: 145g/day (1.8g/kg at 80.5kg)
- Carbs: 450-550g on hard training days
- Hydration: 3L + 500ml per hour of running

## Race nutrition protocol

- Pre-race: oatmeal + banana + coffee 3hrs out
- During: 60-80g carbs/hr via gels + sports drink
- Post: 30-60g protein + carbs within 30 min

## Recovery stack

- Sleep: 7.5-8.5hrs target
- Magnesium glycinate 400mg before bed
- Tart cherry juice post long run
- Ice bath after workouts >15mi
