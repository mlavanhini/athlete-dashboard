# Investment Strategy

Long-term wealth building strategy and personal finance philosophy.

## Core principles

1. Low-cost index funds (Bogleheads approach)
2. Tax-advantaged accounts first (401k → HSA → Roth → taxable)
3. Emergency fund before investing
4. Don't time the market; automate contributions

## Current allocation target

- US stocks: 60% (VTSAX)
- International: 30% (VXUS)
- Bonds: 10% (BND)

## Annual savings targets

- 401k: Max ($23,000 + employer match)
- HSA: Max ($4,150)
- Roth IRA: Max ($7,000) via backdoor
- Taxable: $1,000/month
