# Mindfulness & Focus

Mental performance and stress management practices.

## Daily practice

- 10-minute morning meditation (Headspace, unguided)
- Intention setting post-meditation (3 things I want to feel/do)
- Evening: gratitude journaling (3 items)

## What works

- Meditation > 7 days streak = noticeably calmer at work
- Running is also meditative — long easy runs are my best thinking time
- Reducing news consumption to 15min/day has helped significantly

## What I'm exploring

- Body scan before sleep for better sleep quality
- Walking meditation on easy run days
